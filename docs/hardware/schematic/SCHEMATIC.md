# Draft schematic (v0.2, 17 Oct 2026)

v0.2: input pins follow the pin audit (PR #4). Wheel-speed halls on GPIO34/35, pedal on GPIO36, battery sense on GPIO39, because GPIO36/39 glitch LOW for about 80 ns on every ADC power-up (ESP32 errata) and can't count pulses. GPIO15 is ONEWIRE_TEMP.

A detailed **draft**, drawn before the stock plugs, motor and battery labels and box size are known. Everything marked TBD waits on the photos and measurements. Do not build from it until those are closed.

Sources: [WIRING.md](../WIRING.md), [POWER_CHAIN.md](../POWER_CHAIN.md), [BOM.md](../BOM.md), [include/pins.h](../../../include/pins.h), [include/config.h](../../../include/config.h), and the plug-and-play and per-wheel decisions of 17 Oct.

## Open items (TBD)

1. **Stock plugs J1–J8:** connector family, pin count and which wire is which. Sets every box-edge connector.
2. **Motor pairing in the harness:** if the stock harness joins motors in pairs, J6/J7 become a new 4-lead cable to the motor plugs.
3. **Battery:** voltage, Ah and chemistry. The BTS7960 locks out above 27.5 V; the MDD20A tops out at 30 V.
4. **Motor labels:** current per motor, which settles variant A vs B (20 A vs 43 A per channel).
5. **Box size:** decides variant A (5 boards, bigger box) or B (2 boards, stock-size box).
6. **Key switch rating:** SW1 carries the coil and every rail (about 8 A worst case). If it is rated lower, it drives a 30 A relay instead.
7. **Pedal type:** switch, pot or hall throttle. A switch goes between V+ (3V3_SENSE) and SIG. A 5 V hall throttle needs a divider. Never connect a pedal line that carries 24 V.
8. **Shifter:** whether the stock F/R switch carries motor current or only signals. Only a signal switch can go to the PCF8574.
9. **IBT-2 IS resistor value** (config `IS_RESISTOR_OHMS`).
10. **K1 precharge:** decide after the bench test whether closing on 5 × 470 µF needs a precharge resistor.
11. **AS5600 cable length** to the steering column: over about 30 cm, use its analog OUT on ADS#2 A1 or a bus buffer.
12. **Variant B current sensors:** part and divider.

## Where this draft differs from the current docs

These are deliberate. If they're accepted, WIRING.md and POWER_CHAIN.md should be updated to match (the README asks for the docs and the diagram to agree).

1. **Main current path skips the blade fuse block.** POWER_CHAIN.md draws fuse block → contactor; blade blocks are rated around 30 A per way, so here F1 feeds a stud that goes straight to K1, and the fuse block only carries low-current ways.
2. **Rails are switched by the key.** POWER_CHAIN.md says the rails come from the "key side", but its diagram puts the key only in the coil path. Here the key feeds both, so key off turns everything off and E-stop keeps the ESP32 alive.
3. **Rail fuses sized on the 24 V input side:** 2 A / 3 A / 10 A instead of 2 A / 7.5 A / 15 A.
4. **OLED on 3.3 V, not 5 V.** Its on-board pull-ups would pull the 3.3 V I2C bus up to 5 V.
5. **A3144 hall sensors on 5 V.** They need at least 4.5 V; their open-collector output is still pulled up to 3.3 V, so the ESP32 is safe.
6. **E-stop needs a second NO contact block (S2b)** for ESTOP_SENSE, so firmware reads 0 = pressed as it expects.
7. **Pedal input protection** (series 10 k, 100 k pull-down, clamp, 100 nF) and a **10 nF filter** on each IS line are new.
8. **DS18B20 on GPIO15** (listed as spare), with the 4.7 k pull-up that also keeps the strapping pin HIGH at boot.
9. **DFPlayer on 5V_ACC** rather than the logic rail, so speaker current stays off the ESP32 supply.
10. **Variant B adds a 74LVC08 gate:** the MDD20A has no enable pin, so DRIVE_EN gates the PWM lines instead. It also has no current-sense output, so it needs four external sensors.
